The conversation, continued

The AnalyticsDev.net Webcast Series is a live conversation series for technical analytics practitioners — analytics engineers, data engineers, AI engineers, marketing technologists, and advanced digital analysts. Hosted by Gunnar Griese, it brings together speakers, expert attendees, and future contributors to explore modern measurement, analytics engineering, data activation, AI, and implementation practice.

Each episode continues the AnalyticsDev.net conversation beyond the conference — a practical community space for shared learning, live questions, and deeper reflection. Episodes are broadcast live and later published as a podcast on Spotify.
